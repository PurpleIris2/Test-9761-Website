We’re Daedalus, a FIRST Robotics Competition team learning by making, solving hard problems together, and bringing big ideas to life.

Even as a fourth year team, we are still learning and growing. After going through some major changes with year, 9761 Daedalus Robotics(formally known as LSMSA Robotics) is ready to take on the BIOCORE season!

Our team comes from a small school and town, Nachotiches. Our school, Lousisana School for Math, Science, and the Arts(LSMSA), is a magnet school where kids from across Louisiana come and board to have a college experience during the last three years of high school. Our 10-12th grade students work hard both in robotics and their college level classes to get the best experiences possible!

The name Daedalus Robotics originates from Greek mythological stories, most well known for Icruses story. Daedalus was Icruses' father, a talented and skilled man blessed with his gifts from Athena. He made a labrinth so challenging that not even he could escape if tried. When he was eventually put in the imprisionment with his son, Icrus, by an angry king his wit didn't fail to get him out of his prediciment. Making wax wing for himself and his child, they flew away even though Icruses story shortly ended after his invention worked.

Daedalus symbolizes are hard-working efforts as a team, as all of our memebers are far from home but not far from innovation. As a team, we create more plans and goals for ourselves everyday to help us get somewhere better. We hope as we move through our season, you will help and follow along with us!
